Adds a second bag.

- Press **Left / Right** to switch between bags
- Press **Start** to transfer the selected item to the other bag
- Compatible with Bigger Bags (EriLab) and the TM Pouch mod
- Item order in bag 2 is persisted
- Transferred items land at the top of the destination bag

Story-critical items (e.g. Oak's Parcel) must be in the **active** bag.
