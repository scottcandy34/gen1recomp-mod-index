Completely changes how the HM Fly works.

Instead of opening the town map and selecting a destination, you climb onto the Flying-type Pokémon and freely pilot it across the Kanto overworld. You can land anywhere that is valid terrain.

Gives a much more immersive sense of travel and exploration.
