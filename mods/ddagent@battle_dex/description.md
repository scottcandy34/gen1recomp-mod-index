Gives you Pokédex access during battle.

- Press SELECT while looking at the opponent to open its Pokédex entry.
- A new DEX row appears in the party menu so you can also look up your own Pokémon mid-fight.

Keeps the original Pokédex UI and does not reveal the opponent’s moveset.
