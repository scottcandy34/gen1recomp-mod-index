Every caught Pokémon — not just Pikachu — tracks a hidden 0–255 happiness value.

Happiness can rise or fall based on how you treat the Pokémon (battles, items, walking, fainting, etc.). This brings the classic Gen 2 happiness system into Gen 1 and can be used by other mods or future evolution methods.
