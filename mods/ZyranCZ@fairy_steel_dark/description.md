Adds the Fairy, Steel and Dark types to Gen 1 and provides selectable type-chart generations.

Three new types are introduced with the classic Gen VI relationships. The mod also lets you switch the entire type chart between:

- Vanilla Gen 1
- Gen II (the first major rebalance)
- Gen VI (modern chart including the new types)

Designed to be compatible with the Crystal 251 expansion so the full 251 roster can use the new types correctly.
