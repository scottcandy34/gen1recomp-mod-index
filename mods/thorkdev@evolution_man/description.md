Adds an NPC who will evolve any trade-only Pokémon for a fee of $4000.

No link cable or second player is required. Simply bring the Pokémon that normally needs to be traded (Machoke, Kadabra, Graveler, Haunter, etc.) to the NPC and pay the fee.

Useful for single-player runs that want the full evolutionary lines without trading.
