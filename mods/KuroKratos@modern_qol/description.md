Eight later-generation conveniences behind toggles, all off by default.

**The eight options**

1. **Party-wide EXP** — every party member gains experience, not just the one that fought.
2. **EXP for a capture** — catching a Pokémon awards experience.
3. **Critical captures** — Gen 5-style critical capture chance.
4. **Reusable TMs** — TMs are no longer single-use.
5. **Forgettable HMs** — HM moves can be deleted like any other move.
6. **Run button** — hold B to run in the overworld.
7. **Harmless field poison** — poison no longer drains HP while walking.
8. **Balanced wild encounter slots** — evening out of encounter tables.

Every feature is independently toggleable from OPTIONS and starts **off**, so the vanilla experience is preserved until you turn something on.
