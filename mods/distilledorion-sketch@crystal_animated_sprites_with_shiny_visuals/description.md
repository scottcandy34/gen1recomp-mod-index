Brings Crystal-style animated battle sprites and modern shiny presentation to Gen 1.

Features:
- Full animated front and back sprites in the style of Pokémon Crystal
- Automatic selection of normal vs shiny artwork
- Gen 2-inspired shiny entrance sequence (sparkle + cry)
- Support for the full Johto roster when used with Crystal 251
- Custom trainer portraits, grayscale art sets, and Hall of Fame animations
- Proper Ditto transform visuals and evolution animations

One of the most comprehensive visual overhauls available for Gen1Recomp.
