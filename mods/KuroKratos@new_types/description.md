Adds the DARK, STEEL and FAIRY types with the modern chart, the official retypings, their moves, and ways to learn them.

**What it includes**

- Full Dark, Steel and Fairy type definitions using the modern (Gen VI) type chart.
- Official retypings for existing species where appropriate.
- Signature moves for the new types and learn methods for them.
- Four dedicated NPCs that give TMs / machines for the new moves (placed as new characters, not overwriting vanilla dialogue):
  - Moonblast giver in Mt. Moon 1F
  - Crunch giver in Celadon City
  - Play Rough giver in Fuchsia City
  - Steel machine giver inside the Power Plant
