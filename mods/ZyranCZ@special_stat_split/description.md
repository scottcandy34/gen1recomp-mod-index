Splits the Gen 1 SPECIAL stat into separate Special Attack and Special Defense, matching Gen II onward, and classifies every move as Physical or Special following the Gen IV split.

In the original games every move of a given type was either physical or special. This mod brings the modern system to Gen 1:

- Each Pokémon now has independent SPATK and SPDEF values.
- Moves are scored as physical or special based on their category, not solely on their type.
- Damage calculation, AI decisions and the stats screen all respect the new split.

A large compatibility layer is included so other mods that touch stats or the type chart continue to work correctly.
