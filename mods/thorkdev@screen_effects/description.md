Adds a CRT / film-grain visual style to the game.

Includes:
- Scanlines
- Corner vignette
- Mild edge chromatic aberration
- Animated film grain

All of the effects can be toggled or adjusted from the OPTIONS menu so you can keep a clean look or go full retro-CRT.
