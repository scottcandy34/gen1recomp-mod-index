Allows active and benched party Pokémon to evolve during battle.

When a Pokémon gains enough EXP (or is given an Evolution Stone / Rare Candy) mid-battle it can evolve immediately instead of waiting until the battle ends. This produces a more anime-like experience where a Pokémon can suddenly grow stronger in the middle of a fight.

Compatible with EXP Share Modes mods. Battle and victory music continue seamlessly through the evolution sequence. An OPTIONS toggle controls whether the classic evolution music also plays.

The mod respects the normal evolution rules (level, stones, trade items, etc.) and only changes *when* the evolution is allowed to happen.
