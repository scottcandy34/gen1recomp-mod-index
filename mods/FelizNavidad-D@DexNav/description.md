A DexNav-style tool for browsing and hunting wild encounters.

- View species available on the current map with encounter rates and methods (walking, surfing, fishing separated)
- Register a species and search for it; a roaming indicator helps track it
- Sub-menu to open the Pokédex entry or engage the search
- Compatible with Modern UI, Unique Pokémon Menu Icons, Crystal animated sprites + shiny visuals, Crystal 251, and Voxel mod
- Animated sprites / true-color icons in the DexNav UI when those mods are present
