A Gen 2 style experience bar under your Pokémon's status box, in both the classic and widescreen battle layouts.

In the original Gen 1 UI you only see experience as a number after the battle. This mod draws a proper EXP bar beneath your active Pokémon's HP/status box so you can watch progress fill during the fight, exactly like later generations.

Works with both the original 160×144 battle frame and widescreen battle layouts.
