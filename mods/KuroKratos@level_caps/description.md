Caps party levels at the next gym leader, Elite Four member or Champion, read live from their real rosters.

**How it works**

The mod looks at the actual trainer data of the next major boss you have not yet beaten (gym leader → Elite Four → Champion) and sets a hard level ceiling equal to that trainer’s highest-level Pokémon. Your party cannot gain levels past that ceiling until you defeat them.

Because the caps are derived from the live roster data, any mod that changes gym or E4 teams automatically updates the caps as well.

Ideal for Nuzlocke-style runs or anyone who wants the early- and mid-game to stay challenging.
