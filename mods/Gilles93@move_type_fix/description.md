Corrects a handful of Gen 1 moves that had the wrong type.

Notable fixes:
- Gust becomes Flying (instead of Normal)
- Karate Chop becomes Fighting (instead of Normal)

These are the same corrections that Gen 2 quietly applied. The rest of the type chart is left untouched.
