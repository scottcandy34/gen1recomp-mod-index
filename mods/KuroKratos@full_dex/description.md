Trade evolutions happen by level instead, and every species locked behind a version or a one-off choice gets a place in the wild.

**What it changes**

- Pokémon that normally evolve by trade (Kadabra → Alakazam, Machoke → Machamp, Graveler → Golem, Haunter → Gengar) now evolve by reaching a level instead.
- Version-exclusive and choice-locked species are given wild encounter slots so a single playthrough can obtain all 151.
- Full Yellow support: placements for Weedle, Koffing and Jynx (the three gaps Yellow has that Red/Blue do not).

Verified on both Blue and Yellow — all 151 are obtainable. Species that remain gift / trade / fossil / Game Corner / script sources stay that way.
