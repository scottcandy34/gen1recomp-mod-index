Makes every one of the original 151 Pokémon obtainable in a single playthrough without needing to trade.

Wild encounters, gifts and special events are adjusted so that trade-only and version-exclusive species become available. Full support for Pokémon Yellow is included while still respecting Yellow’s unique starter Pikachu evolution rules.

No major balance changes — the goal is simply to let a player complete the Pokédex without a second cartridge or emulator link.
